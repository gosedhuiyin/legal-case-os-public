# Drafting Contract

## Prerequisites by output

For ordinary internal_review generation: exact task type/audience, selected template or intended generic format, current facts or visible assumptions, source identities/hashes and necessary output constraints. A complete case-state, permanent template registration and G1/G2/G4 are not required. Missing ordinary fields may remain explicit; a material uncertain decision blocks only dependent assertions. Follow [material access and learning](../../../shared/policies/material-access-and-learning.md).

For an explicit court_candidate, additionally require:

- current `G1_strategy` for the exact theory, relief, or defense;
- current-stage `G2_evidence` for every item treated as submitted;
- verified controlling authorities, with material adverse results addressed;
- resolved blocking deep issues or an express lawyer reservation;
- document type, audience, output format, deadline, and length target;
- processing coverage sufficient for every relied-on source;
- template resolution if the user named one.

## Internal drafting record

Record only what the task needs; concise internal records suffice for a simple draft. Complex work adds the useful elements below:

- one-page theory and argument map;
- outline with each section's litigation function and length budget;
- source/evidence/authority mapping;
- assumptions, unresolved questions, adverse facts, and potential admissions;
- artifact audience, input snapshot, version, and dependencies.

## Style and audience

Default to concise, logical paragraphs and a firm but supportable court voice. Preserve the user's usual split: concise submission draft, detailed reserve draft, and pending-confirmation appendix when requested.

A court candidate must not leak AI-workflow language such as “建议、注意、提醒、备注、参考、待核验”, internal IDs, unresolved placeholders, source-control markers, hidden comments, or strategic alternatives. Keep those items in the internal review record, not by deleting the audit trail.

Apply the scoped checks in legal-document-review before delivery; complex/formal work needs an independent pass. Deliver an internal draft with its review limitations rather than waiting for package approval. Drafting cannot grant a review pass or G4; G4 applies only to the exact filing package.
