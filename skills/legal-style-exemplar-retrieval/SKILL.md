# Legal Style Exemplar Retrieval

Follow [material access and learning](../../shared/policies/material-access-and-learning.md). The user may select an uploaded/local document for immediate task use without permanent registration. Authorized knowledge-base discovery and reading are available when needed under [network degradation](../../shared/policies/network-degradation.md); sufficient selected sources do not require a database search.

For an existing named personal asset, resolve its exact catalog/profile version and source hash. Use the approved WritingProfile when current, and read only the source passages needed for the task. For a newly selected file, make a task-local source record and inspect the relevant original passages. Do not scan the whole library or silently register/upgrade a profile.

Return a small useful selection with:

- observed structure, tone, paragraph function and length choices;
- source-supported issue decomposition, evidence ordering and rebuttal methods;
- attributed opinions, their factual/rule premises, limitations and verification status;
- what may guide this task, what must be re-established from current sources, and whether similarity is stylistic, legal or both.

A selected opponent filing, discussion draft or AI draft may be analyzed as that kind of material. It is not thereby an approved final template, reliable authority or accurate statement of this case. Do not attribute a mental process or conclusion that the source does not express. Separate observation of a useful argument from endorsement of its premise.

For multiple examples, identify a layout/structure primary and only useful section-specific auxiliaries. Preserve disagreements that can affect the argument. An attributed old-case opinion can inform comparison; prior names, dates, amounts, evidence and outcomes cannot be silently transplanted into the current matter.

Apply the selected pattern in the current task. Long-term learning is a separate content-confirmed operation under the shared learning policy, with minimal approved source excerpts and versioned provenance. It does not rewrite the global personal style profile or this Skill. If no usable named source is available, state that limit and use authorized supplied material or an explicitly generic style.

Read [the exemplar record](references/exemplar-record.md) for task-local records versus approved reusable records. For a suite, load only the components the user requested.
