# Review Contract

Apply the relevant checks below to the requested audience. An internal draft may have explicit gaps and task-local source records without G1/G2/G4 or full case-state; delivery should state those gaps. A court_candidate must satisfy current strategy/evidence gates and formal citation checks. G4 is separate package approval. See [human approval](../../../shared/policies/human-approval.md).

Review:

- unsupported, inferred-as-fact, or overbroad factual and numerical statements;
- unverified, stale, misquoted, or overstated authorities and missing adverse distinctions;
- source identities/locators, proposed versus submitted evidence, applicable current-stage G2 for a court candidate, and disclosure risk;
- names, roles, case number, dates, amounts, calculations, relief, and attachment consistency;
- element-to-fact logic, burdens, unanswered opponent arguments, and weakest links;
- unnecessary admissions, waiver, overcommitment, confidentiality, or privilege risk;
- stale strategy, source, evidence, authority, template, or input snapshots;
- repetition, disproportionate length, and passages without a litigation function;
- court-candidate AI traces, internal IDs, unresolved placeholders, comments, tracked changes, hidden text, metadata, and draft watermarks;
- page rendering defects, blank pages, clipping, pagination, broken tables, and template displacement.

For a simple internal draft, return only meaningful findings and checks performed. For complex/formal review, include the relevant items below rather than generating empty tables:

1. pass/fail/needs-review status and exact re-review conditions;
2. blocking defects;
3. matters requiring lawyer judgment;
4. one-page control sheet;
5. the highest-value locations for the lawyer to read;
6. source, authority, and evidence exception table;
7. suggested deletions/compression targets and opponent-facing risks;
8. preflight and visual-QA results;
9. recommendations without overwriting the reviewed artifact.

An artifact can pass only when its exact hash and input snapshot were reviewed. A modified or cleaned derivative is a new version and requires a new review.
