# Legal Evidence Gate

Keep three layers separate:

1. indexed source inventory — received material, not automatically evidence;
2. evaluated evidence records and AI recommendations — candidates only;
3. exact lawyer-approved, stage-specific submission set — eligible for packaging.

For each candidate, state what it proves, what it does not prove, source/page support, authenticity and admissibility concerns, adverse content, opponent use, duplication, timing, consequence of withholding, and safer substitutes. Prefer proof-density over volume.

Store the AI recommendation separately from the lawyer decision. Never infer approval from drafting, packaging, “simple case,” or a prior stage. Only an exact current `G2_evidence` approval may move the item into the submission set.

A source-linked client instruction such as “客户就是要提交” is relevant context, not an evidence finding or approval. Preserve the instruction event and its scope while leaving legal relevance, AI recommendation, adverse-risk analysis, and submission status independent; require the lawyer's exact current G2 decision.

“希望只给法院” is a disclosure intent, not a promise. Record intended recipient and service-obligation status, then require verified procedural authority before describing any restricted handling.
