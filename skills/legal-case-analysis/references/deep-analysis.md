# Deep Issue Analysis

Lock analysis to the specified issue and produce:

1. issue ID, exact decision question, trigger, and blocking status;
2. why the issue changes outcome, procedure, relief, or evidence strategy;
3. confirmed, disputed, stated, and inferred facts with source levels and locators;
4. competing legal theories, elements, burdens, and procedural constraints;
5. our strongest argument and its weakest link;
6. the opponent's strongest argument and its weakest link;
7. supporting and adverse authority tasks plus material distinctions;
8. evidence gaps, backfire risks, authenticity or disclosure concerns;
9. current conclusion, confidence, premises, and unresolved uncertainty;
10. facts or authorities that would reverse or materially narrow the conclusion;
11. decision options, tradeoffs, and the smallest next action;
12. return condition for resuming the standard flow.

Also return a one-page control summary and the five highest-value items for the lawyer to inspect. Do not expand one difficult issue into an unlimited whole-matter essay or silently analyze beyond an explicit scope lock.
