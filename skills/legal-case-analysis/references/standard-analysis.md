# Standard Analysis

Default chat presentation:

1. what the matter is and the current objective;
2. the one to three main problems;
3. decisive gaps, adverse facts, or procedural risk;
4. the smallest next action or lawyer decision.

Maintain a fuller internal record with:

- role, stage, forum, relief/defense objective, and read-scope coverage;
- confirmed, disputed, stated, and inferred facts with L1–L4 source levels and locators;
- material timeline and unresolved conflicts;
- elements, apparent burdens, and missing prerequisites;
- our strongest route and the opponent's strongest response;
- evidence red flags for `legal-evidence-gate`;
- controlling-law questions for `legal-authority-research`;
- any issue-level deep trigger and whether it blocks drafting.

Do not make the chat answer long merely because the internal record is detailed. Do not hide a deep trigger to preserve the “simple case” label.
