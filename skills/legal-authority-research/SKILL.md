# Legal Authority Research

Start with the issue, material factual conditions and proposition to test; identify jurisdiction, stage and relevant time as needed. Use [material access and learning](../../shared/policies/material-access-and-learning.md) for common source records and [network degradation](../../shared/policies/network-degradation.md) for access. The configured `law_library` read-only MCP is authorized independently of public internet access. Sufficient selected sources may be read directly.

When library research is needed, retain the user's original question and precise conditions, start with text search, then read full relevant units and context. Add keyword groups or an independent semantic supplement only when it helps resolve a defined gap. Retrieve supporting and materially adverse material; distinguish same-topic grouping from identical holdings.

Uploaded/local cases, rules, author analyses and opposing submissions can support bounded comparison without a database. Mark who made each statement and in what procedural role; do not turn an advocate's argument into a judicial finding. Source-based viewpoints may be learned and used as conditional argument candidates in this task, with long-term saving governed separately by the shared learning policy.

For formal authority use, verify the source identity, actual primary text, scope, relevant validity and quotation location. Never invent or broaden a case number, holding, date, court, or locator. A snippet, unavailable record, old template's citation or model recollection remains a lead. When current validity cannot be checked, deliver the supported internal analysis and identify the affected formal proposition as unresolved.

Complex work returns comparison of facts, procedure, burden, reasoning, disposition and limitations, including the strongest adverse route. Where a formal CompositionSpec is required, provide its ClaimBindings with exact propositions, sources, locators, applicability, support/adverse role and verification status. A simple internal analysis can use compact source bindings instead of full case-state/composition records.

Stop when the material question and important contradiction have adequate support, further searching mainly repeats results, the actual authorized collection is exhausted or verification is blocked. Do not report exhaustive coverage or “no adverse case exists” beyond what was searched.

Read [research method](references/research-method.md) for the search/compare loop and [research record](references/research-record.md) before declaring authority verified. Apply shared [source](../../shared/policies/source-and-citation.md) and [staleness](../../shared/policies/version-and-staleness.md) rules. No research step authorizes a new paid service, corpus mutation or external filing action.
